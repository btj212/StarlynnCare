WAC 388-76-10165 Background checks Washington state name and date of birth
background check Valid for two years National fingerprint background check Valid
indefinitely.
(1) A Washington state name and date of birth background check is valid for two years from the
initial date it is conducted. The adult family home must ensure:
(a) A new DSHS background authorization form is submitted to the department's background check
central unit every two years for each individual listed in WAC 388-76-10161.

This requirement was not met as evidenced by:

Based on record review and interviews, the adult family home failed to ensure a name and date of
birth background check was completed every two years for 2 of 4 current staff. This failure placed
residents at risk for harm from receiving care from caregivers whose criminal backgrounds were unknown.

WAC 388-112A-0200 What is orientation training, who should complete it, and when should it
be completed? There are two types of orientation training: Facility orientation training and
long-term care worker orientation training.

This requirement was not met as evidenced by:

Based on record review and interviews, the adult family home failed to ensure that staff received
the facility orientation necessary to perform their job duties. This failure placed residents at risk
for unmet care needs.

WAC 388-76-10161 Background checks Who is required to have one.
(1) The adult family home must ensure a background check is completed for the individuals named
in this section before they have unsupervised access to residents.

This requirement was not met as evidenced by:

Based on record review, the adult family home failed to keep a background check for one staff
person who had unsupervised access to residents. This failure placed residents at risk.
