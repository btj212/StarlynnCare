Statement of Deficiencies                License #: 757030      Compliance Determination # 52545
Plan of Correction                Blue Orchid Adult Family Home                  Completion Date
Page 13 of 21               Licensee: BLUE ORCHID ADULT FAMILY HOME LLC               01/06/2025

The department completed an inspection of your adult family home on 05/20/2025.

WAC 388-76-10135 Qualifications Caregiver. The adult family home must ensure each
caregiver has the following minimum qualifications:
(7) Has a current valid first-aid card or certificate as required in chapter 388-112A WAC.

This requirement was not met as evidenced by:

Based on observation, interview, and record review the Adult Family Home failed to ensure 3 of 5
staff had valid cardiopulmonary resuscitation and first-aid training. This failure placed all
residents at risk for unqualified staff.

WAC 388-76-10475 Medication Log. The adult family home must:
(1) Keep an up-to-date daily medication log for each resident.

This requirement was not met as evidenced by:

Based on record review the Adult Family Home failed to keep the medication log current for one
resident. This failure placed that resident at risk for a missed dose.

WAC 388-76-10135 Qualifications Caregiver. The adult family home must ensure each
caregiver has the following minimum qualifications:
(4) Has completed the training requirements in effect on the date the caregiver was hired.

This requirement was not met as evidenced by:

Based on observation, interview, and record review the Adult Family Home failed to ensure 3 of 5
staff completed twelve hours of continuing education. This failure placed all residents at risk.
