        NOTICE OF CONDITIONS ON LICENSE
                                        August 20, 2026

Based on the Statement of Deficiencies dated August 7, 2026, the Department of Social and Health
Services imposes the following conditions on the license of Beatrice Adult Family Homes Inc,
License # 753541, located at 19003 52nd Ave W, Lynnwood, Washington.

   •   The Adult Family Home (AFH) Provider, at their own expense, must hire a Registered Nurse
       Consultant (RNC), not currently or previously affiliated with the AFH, and familiar with
       AFH regulations by Tuesday, September 1, 2026.

   •   The RNC must assist the AFH Provider in developing and implementing system to ensure:
       o All newly admitted residents have a completed assessment and completed preliminary
          plan in place.
       o All staff, to include AFH Provider, know how to review and update Negotiated Care
          Plans to match resident’s assessed needs.

   •   The RNC must assist the AFH Provider in reviewing current medication practices and
       developing a standard medication system to ensure:
       o Each resident’s negotiated care plans (NCP) identify the amount of medication services
          they need and will be provided with.
       o Residents receive all medications as required and as prescribed.
       o Medication logs include all prescribed medications and are kept up to date.

   •   The RNC must provide training for all AFH staff, to include the AFH Provider, on:
       o How to identify and monitor residents for changes in conditions.
       o How to identify medical emergencies.
       o How to respond to medical emergencies.

   •   Review and evaluate all current AFH policies and procedures, specifically those related to:
       o Emergency response protocols.
       o Physician Orders for Life-sustaining Treatment
       o Reporting requirements.
       o Incident Log.

   •   The AFH Provider must provide the RNC with a copy of the Statement of Deficiencies dated
       August 7, 2026.

   •   The AFH Provider must provide the name and contact information of the RNC to the
       Department Field Manager upon hire.

   •   The RNC must be available to the Department for questions.
