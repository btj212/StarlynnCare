Dear Licensee:

On August 7, 2026, the Department of Social and Health Services (DSHS), Residential Care
Services completed a complaint investigation at your facility. This letter is formal notice of the
imposition of civil fines, conditions on a license, and continued stop placement order prohibiting
admissions on the license of your adult family home, located at 19003 52nd Ave W, Lynnwood,
by the State of Washington, Department of Social and Health Services, pursuant to the Revised
Code of Washington (RCW) 70.128.160 and 70.128.306; and Washington Administrative Code
(WAC) 388-76-10940.

The civil fines, conditions on a license, and continued stop placement order prohibiting admissions
are based on the following violations of the RCW and/or WAC determined by the department in
your adult family home and described in the attached Statement of Deficiencies (SOD) report
dated August 7, 2026.

Civil Fines

WAC 388-76-10400 (2)(3)(a)(b)(4) Care and services.                                       $500.00

The licensee failed to implement one former resident’s care plan when they failed to
contact the former resident’s Primary Care Provider (PCP) after an unwitnessed fall with
pain. Additionally, the Adult Family Home failed to recognize signs and symptoms of the
decline in the former resident’s functional status as it’s related to a head injury and bone
fracture from an unwitnessed fall. This failure resulted in delayed access to higher level
medical care evaluation, treatment, and contributed to one former resident being
administered discontinued pain medication.

being administered discontinued pain medication.

The licensee failed to ensure a discontinued opioid pain medication (Oxycodone) was not
administered to one former resident after a fall with severe injury. This failure resulted in
the former resident receiving two doses of a discontinued opioid pain medication and
placed the former resident in danger of developing unexpected medical emergency.

The licensee failed to have a system in place to ensure a written Negotiated Care Plan
(NCP) was developed for one resident within 30 days from their admission date. This
failure placed the resident at risk of having unmet care and unrecognized care needs.

WAC 388-76-10340 (1)(2)(3)(4)(5) Preliminary service plan.

The licensee failed to develop a preliminary service plan (PSP) for one resident. This
failure placed the resident’s welfare and safety at risk by not ensuring the Adult Family
Home staff had the information needed to address needs, preferences and specific
problems identified in the resident’s assessment.

The department has determined that the following conditions shall be placed on your adult family
home:

   •   The Adult Family Home (AFH) Provider, at their own expense, must hire a
       Registered Nurse Consultant (RNC), not currently or previously affiliated with the
       AFH, and familiar with AFH regulations by Tuesday, September 1, 2026.

   •   The RNC must assist the AFH Provider in developing and implementing system to
       ensure:
       o All newly admitted residents have a completed assessment and completed
          preliminary plan in place.
       o All staff, to include AFH Provider, know how to review and update Negotiated
          Care Plans to match resident’s assessed needs.

   •   The RNC must assist the AFH Provider in reviewing current medication practices
       and developing a standard medication system to ensure:
       o Each resident’s negotiated care plans (NCP) identify the amount of medication
          services they need and will be provided with.
       o Residents receive all medications as required and as prescribed.
       o Medication logs include all prescribed medications and are kept up to date.

   •   The RNC must provide training for all AFH staff, to include the AFH Provider, on:
       o How to identify and monitor residents for changes in conditions.
       o How to identify medical emergencies.
       o How to respond to medical emergencies.

   •   Review and evaluate all current AFH policies and procedures, specifically those
       related to:
       o Emergency response protocols.
       o Physician Orders for Life-sustaining Treatment
       o Reporting requirements.
       o Incident Log.

   •   The AFH Provider must provide the RNC with a copy of the Statement of
       Deficiencies dated August 7, 2026.

   •   The AFH Provider must provide the name and contact information of the RNC to the
       Department Field Manager upon hire.

   •   The RNC must be available to the Department for questions.

   •   The RNC must submit evidence of AFH Provider training, AFH staff training, and
       policies reviewed to the Department by 5:00PM on Monday, September 21, 2026.
