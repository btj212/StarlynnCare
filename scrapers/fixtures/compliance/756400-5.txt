                 CONTINUED STOP PLACEMENT ORDER PROHIBITING ADMISSIONS

Dear Licensee:

On May 18,2026, the Department of Social and Health Services (DSHS), Residential Care
Services conducted a complaint investigation at your facility. This letter constitutes formal notice
of the imposition of continued stop placement order prohibiting admissions for your adult family
home, located at 1430 Sunset Pl, Mount Vernon, by the State of Washington, Department of
Social and Health Services, pursuant to the Revised Code of Washington (RCW) 70.128.160 and
70.128.306; and Washington Administrative Code (WAC) 388-76-10940.

The continued stop placement order is based on the following violations of the RCW and/or WAC
found by the department in your adult family home, described in the attached Statement of
Deficiencies (SOD) report dated May 18, 2026.

WAC 388-76-10400 (1)(2)(3)(a)(b)(c) Care and services.

The licensee failed to ensure care and services were provided in a manner that actively
supported and maintained two residents quality of life. These failures resulted in a delay in
medical treatment for a resident after they left the AFH unattended and contributed to
another resident experiencing unmet care needs identified in their negotiated care plan.

WAC 388-76-10161 (2)(b)(3) Background checks—Who is required to have.

The licensee failed to ensure one household member had a valid Washington state name
and date of birth background check and one staff completed a national fingerprint
background check. These failures placed all the residents safety at risk when those with
unsupervised access did not have the required background check results.

WAC 388-76-10225 (1)(b)(iii)(2)(a)(c)(f) Reporting requirement.

The licensee failed to ensure that resident incidents for a missing resident, bruising from
unknown causes, and resident altercations, were reported to the Department’s Complaint
Resolution Unit (CRU), resident’s family, health care providers, and case managers for two
residents. This failure resulted in a delayed investigation and delay in medical treatment.

WAC 388-76-10740 (1) Lighting.

The licensee failed to ensure that all bathroom lights in the home were installed and
functionable for six residents. This failure resulted in residents being unable to safely use a
common area bathroom.

WAC 388-76-10750 (5)(a)(c) Safety and maintenance.

The licensee failed to ensure the home was in good repair and that six residents had
consistent heat in the home. This failure resulted in residents not having heat.

WAC 388-76-10455 (2) Medication—Administration.

The licensee failed to ensure that all prescribed medications for one resident were
administered by staff who were trained in nurse delegation. This failure placed the resident
at risk of medication errors and medical complications.

WAC 388- 76-10255 (1)(2) Infection control.

The licensee failed to ensure six residents had supplies available and accessible in the
common use bathroom (Bathroom 2) to wash their hands after using the bathroom, and
when needed. This failure placed residents at increased risk for illness and infection.

The stop placement order prohibiting admissions to your adult family home was effective
immediately upon verbal notice to you on March 19, 2026, in a notice letter dated March 23, 2026.
The stop placement order is continued upon verbal notice to you on May 27, 2026, and confirmed
by certified mail of this letter and the attached Statement of Deficiencies report. The continued stop
placement order prohibiting admissions will not be postponed pending an administrative hearing or
informal dispute resolution process, as is required by RCW 70.128.160(5). The continued stop
placement applies to all new admissions, re-admissions, and transfer of residents.

During the continued stop placement, you may not admit any new resident to your adult family
home. In addition, you may not allow any resident who was absent from the home due to a
temporary non-outpatient stay (not including out-patient treatment) at a hospital, nursing home or
other treatment center to return during the continued stop placement unless you obtain advance
approval from the department. You may request such approval by contacting Nicholette Flynn,
Field Manager, at (206) 348-9350.

Provider Process for Choosing a Panel or Traditional IDR:

You may only choose a Panel IDR if you are disputing three or fewer citations or
enforcement actions. You may choose a Traditional IDR regardless of the number of citations
or enforcement actions you intend to dispute. If you choose a Panel IDR, all documents
supporting your dispute must be submitted within 20 working days after you receive this
letter. For Panel IDRs, the IDR program will not consider any documents submitted after the
20 working day deadline. For Traditional IDR you should submit documents supporting
your dispute at least seven days prior to the date of the IDR meeting.

You can make an IDR request and find directions on the IDR web page at:
http://www.dshs.wa.gov/altsa/idr.
