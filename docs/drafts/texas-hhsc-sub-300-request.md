## Why this stays under $300

Texas Government Code §552.261(a) limits the charge for 50 or fewer pages of paper records to the per-page copy charge, unless the records are in two or more separate buildings or remote storage. Section 552.2615 requires a written itemized statement before a charge over $40. Section 552.267 allows a waiver or reduction when release primarily benefits the public.

The three largest visits (VJG521, U8OF21, and U8OF22) are the ones most likely to push the page count over 50. The email tells HHSC to stop and estimate before producing anything.
